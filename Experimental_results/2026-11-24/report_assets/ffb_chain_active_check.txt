## Interpretation

- The force path is working end to end: publisher -> relay -> `/collision/ffb_command`
  -> adapter -> `applied_magnitude=0.05`.
- `pattern=1` is the directional force; `direction=1` with the box on the right.
  The B r03 archive had the box on the left and `direction=-1`; the sign follows the side.
- `pattern=3` is a vibration/alert pattern (UNKNOWN). The M runs felt this vibration.
- The directional `pattern=1` at 0.05 was not perceptible, while the vibration at 0.05 was.
  This is the basis for the "0.05 directional weight is below perception" judgment.
- The idle samples were `active:false / no_alert` and `applied_magnitude:0 /
  inactive:clear`; those are normal when no WARNING/CRITICAL is present.
